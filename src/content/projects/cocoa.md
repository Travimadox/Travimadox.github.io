## The challenge

I competed solo in the IndabaX South Africa cocoa-contamination detection hackathon on Zindi. The task brought object detection together with a limited local training budget.

## What I did

I trained on an RTX 2050 with 4 GB of VRAM, using Python and PyTorch. Constrained hardware made the training setup part of the practical challenge.

## Result

I finished **5th**, with approximately **0.743 mAP@50**, as recorded in the supplied project brief. These are competition results, not a claim of field deployment or production accuracy.
